# Exercise 1: Matrix Size and Entries

## 1. Problem Statement

Given $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1&3\\0&4&5\end{pmatrix}$ and $B=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-2&3\\4&1\end{pmatrix}$, find their dimensions, entries $a_{12},a_{23},b_{21},b_{32}$, the second row of $A$, and the first column of $B$.

## 2. Theoretical Background and Concepts

A matrix of size $m\times n$ has $m$ rows and $n$ columns. The entry $a_{ij}$ is in row $i$, column $j$. A row vector is horizontal; a column vector is vertical.

## 3. Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

1. $A$ has 2 rows and 3 columns, so its size is $2\times3$. $B$ has 3 rows and 2 columns, so its size is $3\times2$.

2. Reading row and column positions gives $a_{12}=-1$, $a_{23}=5$, $b_{21}=-2$, and $b_{32}=1$.

3. The second row of $A$ is the row vector $(0,4,5)$. The first column of $B$ is the column vector $\begin{pmatrix}1\\-2\\4\end{pmatrix}$.

## 4. Verification and Consistency Checks

Matrix $A$ visibly contains 6 entries arranged in 2 rows and 3 columns; $B$ contains 6 entries arranged in 3 rows and 2 columns. Each selected entry and vector matches its indicated row or column.
